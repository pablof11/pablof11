## Featured project

### [Legal Reasoning Bench: Customs, Trade and Regulatory Law](https://github.com/pablof11/legal-reasoning-bench)

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.24350871.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24350871)

An expert-designed benchmark of professional legal reasoning: six cases across the EU, UK, US and Colombia, in English and Spanish, scored with weighted rubrics and penalty criteria.

- Evaluated with [Inspect](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk), the open-source framework of the UK AI Security Institute.
- Graded by model judges from three developers, with author review of disputed criteria.
- Round 1 measures judge–expert agreement and self-preference bias, as well as model performance.
